Nashik Approves City-Wide Solar Project

The city council of Nashik approved a new solar power project on Monday.
The project will install solar panels on 500 government buildings across the city.
Officials said the solar project will reduce electricity costs by 30 percent.

The mayor thanked the citizens for their support during the public consultation.
Work on the solar panels will begin in January and finish within one year.
